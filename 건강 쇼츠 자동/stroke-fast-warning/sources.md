## Verified Claims Used

- Mayo Clinic says stroke is a true emergency, every moment counts, and people should call emergency services right away if stroke signs appear.
- Mayo Clinic lists FAST signs: face drooping, arm weakness or numbness, speech trouble, and time to call emergency services.
- NIH MedlinePlus/NINDS says stroke signs can include numbness, tingling, slurred speech, confusion, severe headache, and trouble walking or seeing.
- NIH MedlinePlus notes that even if symptoms go away, emergency care is still needed.
- St John Ambulance first aid guidance says not to give a suspected stroke patient anything to eat or drink because swallowing may be difficult.
- American Stroke Association educational material says to call emergency services and not give food, drink, or medication when stroke is suspected.
