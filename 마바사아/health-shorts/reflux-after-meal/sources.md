## Health claims used

- Acid reflux can cause heartburn and sour or acidic regurgitation.
- Lying down after meals and eating close to bedtime can worsen reflux symptoms for some people.
- Lifestyle steps that may help include avoiding meals close to bedtime, staying upright after eating, identifying trigger foods, and limiting foods or drinks that worsen symptoms.
- Seek medical care if symptoms are frequent, severe, persistent, or accompanied by concerning signs such as trouble swallowing, weight loss, or ongoing chest/abdominal pain.
